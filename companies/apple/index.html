Apple

The world's largest hardware-plus-services business: sells premium devices to a >2.5B active installed base, then makes money off that base with a 76%-margin Services layer (App Store, ads, cloud, TV, payments, subscriptions). Arc: from a device company limited by demand to one limited by its own supply of leading-edge chips, shifting its balance sheet and story to the AI-spending era — AI sold as part of the experience, not a standalone feature, with the Mac quietly becoming a box for running AI and agents locally.
