Uber

A marketplace for movement: rides, food and grocery delivery, and freight. It makes money by matching riders with drivers on a two-sided network. The story has shifted from a money-losing aggregator to a profitable, cash-generating platform that wants to be the neutral third-party (3P) demand layer every self-driving-car company routes through.
